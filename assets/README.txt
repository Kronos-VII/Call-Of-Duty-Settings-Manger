Game pictures used by the app. build.bat copies this folder to dist\assets.

  warzone.jpg  bo6.jpg  bo7.jpg  mw4.jpg      game banners and card pictures
  logo.png                                   app logo, top left (square, transparent background)
  app.ico                                    window, taskbar and .exe icon (used by build.bat)

Replace any of them with your own image (same name, jpg or png). Wide images, 1600 px or more, look best.
The app darkens them itself, so bright pictures are fine.
These files are ignored by git on purpose; see .gitignore.
